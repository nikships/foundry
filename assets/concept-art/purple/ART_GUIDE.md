# Foundry Deep-Purple Concept Art Collection

This collection preserves Foundry’s dark industrial blueprint-workcell language while replacing the original orange signal system with a deep Lakers-inspired purple. The working accent pair is `#552583` for core routing and `#6A0DAD` for brighter activity cues; no orange, gold, yellow, pink, magenta, or blue accents are used.

| Artwork | Scene | Suggested placement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `foundry-purple-command-nexus.png` | A central command nexus routes work to six isolated factory cells. | Alternate landing-page hero or architecture section. |
| `foundry-purple-spec-forge.png` | An idea core is transformed into sealed work modules through a vertical precision forge. | Prompt-to-plan, intake, or orchestration section. |
| `foundry-purple-proof-lattice.png` | Evidence modules traverse an inspection lattice toward a radial archive. | Evidence, auditability, or run-history section. |
| `foundry-purple-sentinel-array.png` | A precision machine and robotic arms inspect a single work module. | Gates, automated review, or validation section. |
| `foundry-purple-midnight-grid.png` | A serene distributed factory grid operates after dark. | Closing visual, system-overview canvas, or desktop background. |

All images are 2560 × 1440 PNGs. The left third is intentionally quiet in most scenes to support overlay copy without obscuring the artwork.
